## What happened?
Facts only, the way a neutral witness would describe it.

## Who did it affect, and how?
Think about trust, not just the surface event. If they found out, what would it tell them about me?

## What was I avoiding or protecting?
Shame, a conversation, a judgment, wanting something without having to explain it?

## What did I tell myself to make it okay?
For example, "it's not a big deal" or "it's easier this way."

## Have I done this before?
When, and with whom?

## What do I owe them?
Write the exact words I'd say, with no "but."

## When will I say it?
A specific day and time.

## Next time I feel this urge, what will I do instead?
Name one concrete action.

## Am I punishing myself instead of repairing?
Yes or no. If yes, go back to the "What do I owe them?" question.
