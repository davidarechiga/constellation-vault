# Shark Tank Project Steps

Order of work for the Bocas del Toro surf hostel project (BUS 10, Dave + Itai). Follows the 12-step sequence in [[BUS 10 Shark Tank Business Plan Project — Complete Working Summary]]. Made Nov 16, 2026. No dates yet because the Canvas final deadline is unconfirmed. Set internal deadlines in step 0.

Roles: Dave = finance, design system. Itai = business and operations, market research. Both = marketing, presentation, coordinator/editor.

BUS 10 bans AI-written work and scans for it. Use AI for research, quizzing and feedback on your own writing, not for text you hand in.

## Step 0: Lock the basics (about 30 minutes with Itai)

- [ ] 1. Ask Pete DuBois: final due date, 10 pages or 10 to 12, pitch 10 or 15 minutes, one team upload or each student, rubric point values.
- [ ] 2. Set internal deadlines for every step below and write them in the brainstorm doc's "Internal deadline" column.
- [ ] 3. Each of you fills your own column in the brainstorm doc without looking at the other's, then compare.

## Step 1: Concept (both)

- [ ] 4. Agree on the kind of hostel, and the number of dorm beds and private rooms.
- [ ] 5. Decide where: Carenero first, or confirm another location.
- [ ] 6. Write the mission and vision, and pick the name.

## Step 2: Legal and operations (Itai leads)

- [ ] 7. Pick the legal structure and where the business is registered.
- [ ] 8. Settle how a foreigner can own a business in Panama, including concession versus titled land.
- [ ] 9. Decide the building (lease, buy or build), staff, and surf services in-house or with a local surf school.

## Step 3: Market research (Itai leads)

- [ ] 10. Find the NAICS code for hostels and traveler accommodation.
- [ ] 11. Fill in the competitor table.
- [ ] 12. Collect tourist numbers and the Dec to Mar surf season, with APA source notes as you go.

## Step 4: Marketing, the four Ps (both; heaviest-graded)

- [ ] 13. Product and unique selling point.
- [ ] 14. Price compared with competitors.
- [ ] 15. Place: booking sites and their commissions.
- [ ] 16. Promotion, and the first target group.

## Step 5: Finance (Dave leads, instructor's Excel template)

- [ ] 17. Make the startup cost list from the property and equipment numbers.
- [ ] 18. Set high and low season prices and occupancy.
- [ ] 19. Build the year-1 income statement.
- [ ] 20. Decide the investor ask, what investors get, and the valuation check.

## Step 6: Risks (both)

- [ ] 21. Write the biggest risks and a response for each.

## Step 7: Assemble and check

- [ ] 22. Write the executive summary last, on one page.
- [ ] 23. Add the cover, table of contents and APA references.
- [ ] 24. Check that revenue, expenses and the ask match across the plan, spreadsheet and slides.
- [ ] 25. Build the pitch slides and rehearse to time.
- [ ] 26. Upload all three files to the Module 11 assignment.
