# Bocas Hostel Research

## 1. Dorm and private room prices

| BudgetYourTrip | $14 (23 hostels) | $20 (32 hostels + guesthouses) | Private average includes guesthouses |

- Bambuda Lodge (the main party hostel) has dorms from about $11–13.
- Prices rise in high season, on weekends and on holidays like New Year's Eve.
- **Range to work with:** dorm $12–20, private $40–90. Pick one number and use it the same way in the plan, the spreadsheet and the slides.

## 2. Tourist numbers and busiest months
- **Panama overall:** 3 million international visitors in 2025, up 8.4% (President Mulino, via Casa Solution).
- **First half of 2026:** 1,755,998 visitors, up 17.4% from the year before.
- **Bocas province:** more than 100,000 visitors a year (national and foreign), the 2nd most-visited place in Panama, bringing in about $50M a year (Panamá América, ATP regional director). ⚠️ Old article with no year shown. Use only with that caveat.
- **Busiest months:** December and January, which are also the biggest swell months.
- Europeans: 308,857 visitors to Panama in 2025 (+8.3%). Tour operators say Bocas and Boquete draw European interest after Panama City and the canal.
- Tourism officials name Bocas among the most-visited destinations for foreigners.
- ❓ **Gap:** no official 2025–26 visitor count for Bocas alone. Ask whether citing province-level ATP data is acceptable.

## 3. Surf season
- **Main season:** December to March (some sources say through April). Biggest swells come in Dec–Jan.
- **Mini season:** June to August, with July the strongest month. It's low tourist season, so the lineups are emptier.
- **Off season:** roughly Sept–Nov and May. Good for diving and snorkeling, which could be your "flat surf" answer.
- **Main breaks:** Bluff (heavy shore break, advanced), Paunch (reef, Isla Colón), Carenero Point (reef, beginners learn nearby), Silverbacks (Bastimentos; heavy at size, mellow when small).
- 90% of the surf is on three islands: Colón, Carenero and Bastimentos.
- **Key point:** surf season and tourist high season overlap (Dec–Mar). That's good for high-season occupancy and a risk in the off months.

## 4. Occupancy rates
- **Bocas del Toro hotels: 70.4% average in 2026.** That's the highest region in Panama, ahead of Panama City at 67% (PROMTUR / CoStar, 146-hotel sample, reported Aug 2026).
- Panama nationally: 56% in 2025, 67.6% in the first half of 2026. The hotel association projected 58–60% for 2026.
- ⚠️ These are **hotel** figures, not hostel figures, and the exact months weren't published. No hostel-specific occupancy source was found for Panama.
- **Takeaway:** use separate high- and low-season occupancy numbers instead of one flat 70%.

## 5. Foreigners owning or running a business in Panama
- Foreigners **can own 100%** of a Panamanian company. No government approval is needed for foreign investment.
- **Retail trade is reserved for Panamanians.** A hostel is lodging, not retail, but selling merchandise or running a shop in-house could raise retail questions. Flag it.
- **Working in your own business needs a work permit.** You can own it without residency, but you can't legally work in it day to day without the right visa or permit. This answers the "who runs it from California?" question: a local manager.
- **Common structures:** S.A. (corporation) or S. de R.L. (LLC).
- **Steps:** register the company → get a tax ID (RUC) from the tax office (DGI) → file the operating notice (Aviso de Operación) through PanamaEmprende → get a municipal license → register with the tourism authority (ATP) → register employees with Social Security (CSS).
- **Taxes:** hotels charge a 10% tourism tax on top of the 7% sales tax (ITBMS), per a relocation firm. Confirm that if you use it.

## 7. Booking-site commission rates
| Platform | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hostelworld | 15% standard for new hostels | Paid upfront as the guest deposit. Hostels can choose 10–25%; paying more ranks you higher. |
| Booking.com | 10–25%, average about 15% | Plus 1.1–3.1% for payment processing. Programs like Genius and Preferred Partner add more. |

- **Takeaway:** budget **15–18%** commission on online bookings, and plan to push direct bookings (your own site, Instagram, walk-ins) to save it. That connects to your 4 Ps "Place" section.
