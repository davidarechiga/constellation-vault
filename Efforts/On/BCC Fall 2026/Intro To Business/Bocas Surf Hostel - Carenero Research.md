# Bocas Surf Hostel: Carenero Research

Part of the [[BUS 10 Shark Tank Business Plan Project — Complete Working Summary]]. Team: Dave and Itai Weinberg. Collected Nov 16, 2026 from web listings and travel guides. Sources disagree in places, so confirm with a local surf school, water taxi driver or broker before putting numbers in the plan.

The team brainstorm (Google Doc, "Shark Tank Brainstorm Bocas del Toro Surf Hostel") ranks Isla Carenero first as the location.

## Surf access from Carenero

### Beginner and intermediate

| Break | Level | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| Carenero Point | Intermediate | About a 5-minute walk, per a Carenero lodging listing |
| Old Man's (Carenero) | Beginner to intermediate | On Carenero itself. The inside gets very shallow. |
| Black Rock | Beginner to intermediate | A few hundred meters off the island: a 3 to 5 minute boat or paddle |

Black Rock is the most-cited learner wave, but it is an offshore reef. A beginner lesson from the hostel is a short walk plus a short paddle or boat hop, not a pure walk.

### Advanced (by boat)

| Break | Level | Boat time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dumpers | Advanced | About 10 min from Bocas Town | Sharp, shallow reef |
| Playa Bluff | Advanced | About 5 to 10 min by boat; about 10 km by road | Sources conflict on boat time |
| Paunch | All levels, more forgiving | Not firmly sourced | Quick water taxi from Carenero lodging |
| Silverbacks | Expert, big wave | 25 to 40 min | The outlier |

Add about 1 to 5 minutes for the Carenero to Bocas Town water taxi, since the times above are quoted from town. Season runs roughly December through March or April.

For the pitch: "about 10 minutes to the advanced breaks, 25 to 40 for the big-wave spot."

## Property for sale on Carenero

No hostel found for sale on Carenero. Closest matches are small hotels and B&Bs. All unverified.

- B&B concession, $740,000. 5 bathrooms, built 2016, 386 m² lot, listed March 2026 (freshest).
- Over-water hotel, about $695,000 to $699,000. 21 bedrooms on 0.35 acres, one listing mentions a $100K price cut. Closest fit for a hostel layout (dorms plus privates).
- Over the Caribbean Sea B&B, about $620,000. 8 bedrooms, 6 bathrooms. One page last updated April 2024, so it may be stale.
- Sold or pending, not available: a $795,000 beachfront B&B (sold) and a $975,000 beachfront lodge (pending).

An income-generating hostel is for sale on Isla Colón (price not shown), which is the better comparable for revenue numbers.

Open question for the legal section: a "concession" and over-water property is a different kind of land tenure than titled land. This ties to the brainstorm research item on rules for foreigners owning or running a business in Panama.
