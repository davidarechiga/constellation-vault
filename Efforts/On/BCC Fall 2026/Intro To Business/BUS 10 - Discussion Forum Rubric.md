# Discussion Forum Evaluation Rubric (15 points)

| Evaluation Criteria | Needs Improvement | Capable | Excellent | Outstanding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Quality of Initial Post** | **2 pts** – Did not demonstrate comprehension of the assigned reading or topic, or did not apply critical thinking in the initial post. | **4 pts** – Demonstrated a little comprehension of the assigned topic and a little critical thinking. | **5 pts** – Demonstrated some comprehension of the assigned topic and applied reasonable critical thinking. | **6 pts** – Demonstrated comprehension of the assigned reading or topic and applied critical thinking. |
| **Quality and Relevance of Question(s) to Peers** | **0 pts** – Failed to post a question, or posted a question that lacked any relevance to the assigned reading or topic. | **1 pt** – Question lacked critical thinking and had little relevance to the assigned topic. Posed to the entire class. | **2 pts** – Applied reasonable critical thinking in a question reasonably relevant to the assigned topic. Posed to the entire class. | **3 pts** – Applied critical thinking in a thoughtful and relevant question. Posed to the entire class. |
| **Quality of Response(s)** | **0 pts** – Failed to post, or posted a response lacking substance and relevance. | **1 pt** – Somewhat relevant response but lacked substance; a little comprehension of the posting. | **2 pts** – Some comprehension of the posting and some critical thinking in a substantive response. | **3 pts** – Comprehension of the posting and critical thinking in a substantive response. |
| **Due Dates Met** | **0 pts** – No postings, or postings made after the second due date. | **1 pt** – One posting met a due date, but one or more missed both due dates. | **2 pts** – First due date missed, but all postings finished before the second due date. | **3 pts** – Initial posting, question, and response all posted before their respective due dates. |
| **Total** | **15 points max** | | | |

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Initial post shows comprehension + critical thinking (6)
- [ ] Post a relevant, thoughtful question to the **whole class** (3)
- [ ] Substantive reply to peers — not just "I agree" (3)
- [ ] Hit both due dates (3)
