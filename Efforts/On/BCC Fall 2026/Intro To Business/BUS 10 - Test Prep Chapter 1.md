## Section 1

### 1. What is the difference between revenue and profit?
- **Revenue** is the total amount of money a business takes in during a given period by selling goods.
- **Profit** is the amount of money a business earns above and beyond what it spends for salaries and other expenses.
- The difference: revenue is the base at which profit and loss are determined.

### 2. What is the difference between standard of living and quality of life?
- **Standard of living** is the amount of goods and services people can buy with the money they have.
- **Quality of life** is the general well-being of a society in many more aspects than just money.
- The difference: standard of living is based on money; quality of life is based on personal satisfaction.

### 3. What is risk? How is it related to profit?
- **Risk** is the time and money an entrepreneur spends to run a business without proven profits.
- Great risks most likely means great profit in all industries.
