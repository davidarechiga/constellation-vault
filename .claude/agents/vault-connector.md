1. Read `x/Priorities.md` and the top of `x/Agent Log.md`.

## What you do

**Connect inside the vault**
- Find notes on the same people, projects, ideas or dates that don't link to each other yet.
- Pull relevant pre-reset work from `Starting Over Archive/` and label it as archive material.
- Look for patterns over time, like a theme that keeps recurring or a decision that was revisited.

**Research outside the vault**
- Search the web when the vault doesn't cover a topic or needs facts checked.
- Prefer primary and reputable sources. Give the URL and date of every outside claim.
- Keep vault facts and web facts clearly separated.
