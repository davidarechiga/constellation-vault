# BUS 10 – Course Outline by Module/Week

> [!note] The instructor reserves the right to change the course outline, content and/or schedule as needed to accommodate class progress or unforeseen disruptions.

"UB" = *Understanding Business* (McGraw-Hill), the official textbook. Objective codes (O-#) and SLOs refer to [[BUS 10 - Syllabus#Course Objectives]].
