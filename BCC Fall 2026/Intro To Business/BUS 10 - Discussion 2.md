# Discussion 2 – How the US Compares to the World

Graded with [[BUS 10 - Discussion Forum Rubric]].

## What do you think about the US standing in the world, based on these factors?

The low ranking of the United States on the best countries for raising a family in 2020 list leads me to think this low quality of life is a reflection of the tax-breaking federal policies that have been backed by corporate political donors. It's well known that the rich are getting richer as there have been massive gains in profit over the last couple decades and major tax cuts to businesses and the wealthy leading to much of the money being to the most wealthy families in the United States. According to these numbers: the quality of life is significantly lower than other countries because these other countries are investing their money back into their community to make sure their citizens have enough money for basically living.

## What are the implications for US citizens' day-to-day life?

Looking at the numbers, day to day life is challenging and expensive just to have our basic needs like healthcare and childcare. US Citizens are more likely to be pushed to work longer hours with no monetary incentives leading to burnt out with no relief of a paid vacation. Not only is day to day life challenging, there is really no built-in safety net for United States citizens. This lack of economic policy leaves us vulnerable to health crises or economic down-turns where even just one event is incredibly impactful to quality of life.

## What do these findings say about US political institutions and people of power who arguably have the responsibility to protect our country and the well-being of its citizens?

The findings outlined in these studies paint a clear picture of what our politicians value: more money in fewer hands. The Gross Domestic Product of the United States is one of the highest in the world but our government has chosen to unevenly distribute across the wealthiest of our citizens instead of evenly across our family welfare programs. Our country is falling behind in terms of protecting its people.

## What needs to change? How does that happen?

The US needs to incentivise learning trades, regulate AI and let it work for us. We then can institute a Universal Basic Income, make all public services subsidized by tax dollars including healthcare, child care, education, and basic needs. Honestly, there can still be rich people who earned it from their business but we need to close the gap between people who don't have enough and people how have to much.
